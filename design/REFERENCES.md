# Boutique design direction

The supplied storefront banner is the primary visual reference: navy elegant lettering, fuchsia script, pink blossoms, a fine gold wreath and the tagline “Wear your Confidence”. The reconstructed logo follows that reference.

Online references reviewed:

- https://sabyasachi.com/ — restrained fashion navigation and image-led collection presentation.
- https://www.torani.in/ — occasion and garment categories that help visitors explore couture.
- https://www.anavila.com/home/ — editorial textile presentation and craft storytelling.
- https://srivallidesigners.com/ — blouse categories and a direct consultation flow for a Chennai boutique.

The implementation uses an original navy, fuchsia and ivory theme, Cormorant Garamond and Manrope typography, numbered service links, fine borders and an asymmetric fashion layout. No template or copied brand assets are used.

The reconstructed brand asset and two fashion/embroidery concept images were generated for this website. Concept images are captioned as design inspiration and are not represented as actual completed garments or customer photographs. Replace these with the boutique's own photographs when available.

Fonts are self-hosted. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included in dist/assets/fonts.
